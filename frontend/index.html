How to check the claim

Opus reads your claim and study description, picks the columns and the study design, runs the checks it needs, and judges what the numbers mean. Fixed rules then make sure the verdict never claims more than the interval shows.

With a sentence, we audit it. Without one, we explore what may cause what.
